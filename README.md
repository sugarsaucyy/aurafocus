# AuraFocus

AuraFocus is a student-focused productivity and well-being app designed to help users stay focused, build consistent habits, and take care of their emotional well-being.

The app combines focused work sessions with simple mood tracking and supportive features to create a calmer and more intentional productivity experience.

## Features

- **Focus Timer** — Start focused study sessions and stay present.
- **Mood Check-In** — Track how you're feeling throughout your journey.
- **Streak System** — Build consistency by maintaining your focus sessions.
- **Session Tracking** — Keep track of completed focus sessions.
- **Supportive Experience** — Encouraging messages designed to make productivity feel less stressful.
- **Personalized Journey** — A user-focused experience that grows with your progress.
